Folio Music Privacy Policy

Effective date: (release date)

Folio Music (the "App") is provided by the developer Ruanote (the "Developer"). This policy explains what information the App handles.

1. Summary

The App does not collect your personal information on any server of the Developer. The Developer operates no servers, accounts, ads, or analytics tools for the App.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device only and is never sent to the Developer.

  • Information about the music files you import (title, artist, album, length), album and song settings, lyrics, stamps, and effect ranges
  • Play-time records and statistics
  • App settings (language, view mode, timer, and so on)
  • Processed audio created by the App
  • Backup files you create yourself (you choose where they are saved)

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes the App's information on the device. Backup files must be deleted by you.

3. Payments

Payments for the Pro subscription and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. The Developer does not receive or store card numbers or other payment details. The App only checks through the Google Play Billing Library whether you have a purchase (a subscription or the lifetime purchase). Information that Google processes for payments is governed by Google's privacy policy.

4. Contact email

When you tap "Contact us" in Settings, your email app opens with the recipient, subject, app version, and device information filled in. The email is sent only if you send it yourself, and then your email address and what you wrote reach the Developer. Messages are deleted after they are no longer needed to handle your inquiry.

5. Permissions

  • File access: the App reads only the music files and folders you choose (through the system file picker).
  • Notifications and foreground service: used for background playback and the playback notification.
  • Wake lock: used so playback continues while the screen is off.

6. Sharing and transfers

The Developer does not share your information with third parties. Because the Developer receives no such information, there are no international transfers. Payments are processed by Google.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Your rights

The only personal information the Developer may hold is a contact email you sent. To ask to see or delete it, contact us below.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
